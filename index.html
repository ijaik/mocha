Our Roasting Philosophy

From Karnataka hills to our Jaipur roastery.

Great coffee begins at high altitudes under shade trees. We partner directly with multi-generational estates in Chikmagalur and the Western Ghats to procure traceable micro-lots.

Inside our C-Scheme roastery, each origin receives its own custom roast profile to celebrate innate notes of wild berries, cocoa nibs, caramel, and warm spices. Never over-roasted; always brewed at peak flavor.
