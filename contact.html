Visitor Information

Frequently asked questions.

Helpful tips before stepping into Mocha Café & Roastery.

Do I need a reservation to visit?

Walk-ins are always warmly welcomed! However, for weekend mornings, groups larger than 4, or coffee cupping sessions, reserving in advance guarantees you avoid a wait during peak hours.

Can I work on my laptop from the café?

Yes! Our indoor roastery lounge is equipped with dedicated charging sockets beneath the banquettes and high-speed fiber WiFi (100 Mbps). We ask that patrons order coffee or food every couple of hours.

Can I bring my pet?

Yes! Our outdoor sandstone courtyard is completely pet-friendly. We have fresh water bowls and shaded spots for your pets. Please keep them on a leash for everyone’s comfort.

Do you grind coffee beans for home equipment?

Yes, complimentary custom grinding is included with every 250g bag purchase. Just tell our barista whether you brew on an Espresso machine, Moka Pot, Aeropress, V60, or French Press.
